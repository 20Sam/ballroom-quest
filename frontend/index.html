    Ballroom Quest

    Learn your figures before the lesson. See the footwork from the dancer's own eyes, play it to the beat, and let three AI helpers coach you for competition.

    1. Walk Evans Hall Ballroom with WASD or the arrow keys. Four glass classroom drums hold one studio per style: Standard, Smooth, Latin and Rhythm. Walk in and press E.
    2. Combine figures with number keys 1–9. The chain turns red if a link isn't danceable.
    3. Play with ↑ forward, ↓ back, ← → side, Space close or replace, on the beat.
    4. Ask for help: Coach Marisol reviews your routine, the Drill Board turns lesson notes into drills, and the Planner builds your plan for competition.
